The late and great journalist, Bill McLaughlin, of Saranac Lake submitted the following to the Syracuse Newspapers and it was published in the "Amazing Facts" series; back in the 1950's.

    Sgt. Bernard Brown of Saranac Lake, N.Y. a US Marine in World War II
  • was born in 1913
  • was overseas 13 months, 13 days
  • left Guadalcanal Oct. 13 in boat 13
  • landed at Japanese - held Bougainville in boat 13
  • was evacuated Jan. 13 to a mobile hospital---
  • where he found himself in bed 13
  • Incidentally, Brown,with only 4 men, killed 74 of the enemy, who attacked their position at Bougainville.
