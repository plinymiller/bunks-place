Although not a "rocker", Saranac Laker Phil Klein is a great musician and entertainer. He is Professor of Music Emeritus at Onondaga Community College in Syracuse, a solo pianist, band pianist, composer, arranger and raconteur. Along with these pursuits he is also in the process of putting together a book based on his memories of Saranac Lake. Give a listen to some of Phil's stuff and see if you are as hooked as I am. Phil was a student of the late-great Lorreto Leonard, who taught piano on Park Avenue for many years.

CLONING COOPER
L-R: Steve Hardy, Josh Clement, Bruce Arnold, Brian Lewis, Rob Stout and Walt Lewis

Cloning Cooper's first CD is a must-have, especially if you're an Adirondacker and particularly if you're a Saranac Laker. It's hard to pick a personal favorite because every track on it is great. Not only is the music and editing outstanding but there is a lot of thought behind the lyrics. I was impressed by the sound and message behind "SAGITTARIUS", a tribute to the average guy, "BIDING MY TIME", a statement about the local political situation and "THE INNER CRITIC". (The lead-in is an actual scanner sound bite of a notorious SLPD incident.)

The track, "PAMPLONA", has a distinct Spanish accent and it's fitting that the release of the album coincides with the eight days of the "running of the bulls" in Pamplona, Spain. This daredevil event was popularized by Hemingway in, "The Sun Also Rises".

"We each run our own personal "PAMPLONA" every day of our lives". says Josh. "We try to keep one step ahead of the bulls of financial failure, health worries, family concerns and then there's that secret bull we keep hidden deep inside."

"That's the one that will get you.", says Rob.

"Ole!" shout Walt and Brian in unison.

The offbeat final track, "I DON'T GET DRUNK ANYMORE", is guaranteed to leave you laughing. If you didn't make it to town for the CD release show at THE WATERHOLE on July 11th you can get the CD at: AMAZON.COM. and CD BABY
